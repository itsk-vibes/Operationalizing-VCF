Step 2 of 6

Build the swimlane

Rows are teams, columns are phases. Add a step wherever a team does something, then drag steps between cells as the customer corrects you. Click any step to open its detail. Start from the baseline template if you want a straw man to react to, which is usually faster than starting from an empty grid.

Teams

One row each
Cells with a teal underline are steps you have already flagged as automatable in step 4. A dashed border means the step runs in parallel and is off the critical path, so it adds no lead time even though it still consumes someone's day.
Step 3 of 6

Time every step

Two numbers per step. Work is hands on keyboard. Wait is everything else: sitting in a queue, waiting for the next team to pick it up, waiting for Thursday's CAB. Type durations naturally, so 45m, 4h, 3d or 1.5d all work. The bar underneath rebuilds as you type.
