Why this tracker exists

See what's in use, spot what's untapped, and turn a platform into realized value.

Every cell in the grid below tells a small story about one organization and one VCF capability. Read across the grid and a bigger picture appears: where the platform is already working for the customer, and where capabilities they've paid for are sitting idle. That contrast is the conversation starter.

▲
The goal is movement to the right. Orange and yellow cells are not failures, they're openings. Each one marks a capability the customer already owns but isn't yet getting value from. The tracker makes those openings visible so the right conversation, enablement, or use case can move a cell toward green and the customer toward realized value.
